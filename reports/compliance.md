## The two blocking items, precisely

**Item 8 — golden set.** 200 real, stratified examples exist and the sampling is
documented and reproducible. What does not exist is a *human* label on any of
them. The current labels are independent LLM proposals which agree with the
training rules only 27% of the time, and the disagreement is concentrated almost
entirely in the catch-all class (9% agreement there vs 63.6% elsewhere), because
the proposing model assigns that class to 6.5% of messages where the rules assign
67%. Neither source is ground truth. Until a human arbitrates, every golden-set
metric is a measurement of label disagreement rather than of the agent.

Fix: `python -m src.review_golden_set` (~20 min). The tool disables the
accept-default on disagreeing rows specifically so the bias cannot be imported by
holding ENTER.

**Item 12 — judge/human agreement.** The protocol is implemented: 25 replies
sampled with a fixed seed, rated blind with the judge's score hidden, then exact
agreement, within-1 agreement, Spearman ρ and Cohen's κ on "acceptable ≥ 4". It
has never been run because it needs a human rater. `reports/human_agreement.md`
prints a NOT COMPLETE banner and refuses to emit a figure below n=10.

Fix: `python -m src.human_agreement --rate` (~10 min).

Both are deliberately left visible. Filling them with AI-generated ratings would
have produced two green ticks and a dishonest submission.
